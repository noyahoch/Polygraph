Our pilot · September 14–15

Combine scores, rather than merge graphs?

Four separate detectors at layers 3, 6, 9 and 12; compare their stack with the final-layer detector.

AUROC in seed 7. Late diagnostic.

Our replication · September 16

Does that gain repeat?

Repeated the exact split, architecture and 20-epoch budget with seeds 17 and 27.

Yes: the stack beat the final-layer control in both new seeds.

What we can say: combining these layer detectors gives a small, repeatable gain over the final-layer detector alone.

What we cannot say: graphs are necessary, or this beats the best graph-free method. Simple averaging was slightly better than the learned stack.

Each experiment has its own comparator and evaluation setup; their absolute scores are not a shared leaderboard. The interval above resamples photographs, conditional on the fitted models—not a guarantee across new training seeds.
