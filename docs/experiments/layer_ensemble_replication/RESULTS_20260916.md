## Uncertainty and claim limits

Exactly 2,000 bootstrap draws used RNG seed `20260914`, resampling `image_id`
source photographs. All nine views inherit the same photograph multiplicity,
and draws are shared across methods and seeds. Percentiles use NumPy's linear
method. No undefined primary draws occurred.

The intervals describe source-image sampling uncertainty **conditional on the
fitted detectors and heads**. Sample SD across two or three observed seeds is
descriptive; neither quantity estimates robust population-level training-seed
uncertainty. Development-data reuse remains a limitation.

The results support combining these layer-specific detectors in this setting.
They do not isolate complementary layer information from four-versus-one model
capacity or generic ensemble benefits. All detectors are GNNs; this experiment
does not prove message-passing or graph-topology necessity, or establish
language-model hallucination results. Twenty epochs do not guarantee convergence.

hardware upgrade or HF upload occurred. The original September 14 official
