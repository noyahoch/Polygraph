# September 10: results and reproduction

All seven arms and five seeds completed training, the registered freeze, test scoring and analysis on September 11, 2026. All 35 fits and the complete evaluation have a verified private Hugging Face backup. This page transcribes the existing SHA-verified outputs; no analysis, training or scoring was rerun to write it.

The primary full-graph minus full-set AUROC gap is **0.0062325320**, with **95% interval [0.0026182901, 0.0098357786]**. The interval is positive but crosses the registered practical margin of **0.005**. The practical size of the benefit is therefore unresolved; this does not establish general GNN superiority. The five seed-wise gaps include one negative value.

These are arithmetic means of five seed-wise AUROC contrasts, not AUROC computed after averaging model scores. The 2,000 paired bootstrap resamples use original photographs (`image_id`), preserving all nine versions of each photograph and the same multiplicities across arms and seeds. Intervals describe photograph-sampling uncertainty conditional on these five fitted seeds; they are not a broad account of training randomness or unseen corruption families. Secondary intervals are not multiplicity-corrected superiority claims.

The MSP≥0.9 slice has 5,015 records, 443 distinct photographs with an error and 745 with a correct prediction. It meets the registered support floor of 200 photo groups per outcome; a photograph can occur in both outcome sets, and passing the floor does not guarantee power.

Rewiring changed a mean **0.5039636671** of edges over the full development cohort, below the unchanged **0.80** criterion. Structural construction and the explicit admission passed; mixing quality remained **FAIL / non-diagnostic**. The [pre-test decision](REWIRING_DECISION.md) retained all graphs and all 35 fits without tuning the 2E/20E swap budgets or random stream. The contrast cannot establish topology removal, structural necessity, equivalence, or topology's causal role. Endpoint sets retain attributed relationships, and rewiring changes destination/attribute alignment as well as paths.
